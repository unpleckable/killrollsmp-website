What is KillRoll SMP?

Minecraft's Most Competitive Event

KillRoll SMP is a one-week Minecraft PvP server running daily from 4:30–8:00 pm. Every player starts with Protection III armour, and each kill earns you a random enchantment (level 1–5) on your held item.

Enchantments can appear on any item, with unique effects and three custom enchants: Health, Lifesteal and Swiftness. Every enchantment and level combination is unique to one player, so fight, collect upgrades and become the strongest player on the server!
